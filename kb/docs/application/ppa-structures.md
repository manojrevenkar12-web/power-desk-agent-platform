# PPA structures and how we value them

**Pay-as-produced (PaP).** We buy whatever the plant produces at a fixed strike and sell it in
the day-ahead market. We carry price, shape and volume risk. P&L per period is the market value
of the delivered volume minus volume times strike.

**Capture price.** The generation-weighted average day-ahead price over the period. The capture
rate is the capture price divided by the baseload average. Solar capture rates fall as more
solar is built, because solar produces when everyone else does: the cannibalisation effect.

**Shape discount.** Baseload average minus capture price: what the production profile costs
against a flat delivery.

**Negative-price clause.** No delivery and no payment in quarter hours with a negative day-ahead
price. It protects the buyer from paying the strike for energy that is worth less than nothing.

**Imbalance cost.** We sell the forecast volume day-ahead; deviations are settled at the
imbalance price. Wind portfolios carry more imbalance cost than solar.

Always state which period, which zone and which PPA a number refers to.
