# Weekly research note, week 30

German day-ahead prices spent the week oscillating between a deep solar trough at midday and a
steep evening ramp. On sunny days the trough was set by exports and storage absorbing the
surplus, and on the brightest Sunday renewables curtailed at zero for several hours.

Our view: the evening ramp is where the value is. Gas set the price in the evening peak on most
days, and carbon above 80 EUR/t keeps that peak expensive. Batteries that can do two cycles on
days with a morning and a midday dip are worth looking at; single-cycle assets leave value behind.

Wind was below forecast midweek and the system was short; imbalance prices spiked well above
intraday. Portfolios that sold their wind forecast day-ahead paid for it.
