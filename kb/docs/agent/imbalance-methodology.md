# Imbalance risk check, methodology

Use the PPA's realised imbalance cost over the period as the baseline. For the stress, apply an
extra forecast error of 5 percent of output in the direction that hurts in every quarter hour,
priced at the absolute gap between imbalance and day-ahead prices. Report both, and say that the
stress is a sensitivity, not a forecast. Flag a PPA when the stress adds more than a third to
the realised cost.
