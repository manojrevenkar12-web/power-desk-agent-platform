# Berlin desk hedging strategy

We hedge the expected output of our German PPAs with month baseload futures. The target hedge
ratio for the front month is 70 to 90 percent of expected PPA output; above 110 percent we are
net short and must reduce. Peak futures cover the solar shape only in summer months.

Current stance: we are over-hedged in July against the PPA output; do not add baseload sales for
July and August before the PPA forecast is updated.
