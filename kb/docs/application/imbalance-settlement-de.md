# Imbalance settlement in Germany (reBAP)

Every balancing responsible party settles the difference between its schedule and what was
actually produced or consumed at the uniform imbalance price, the reBAP. It is set per quarter
hour, is the same in all four German control areas and is symmetric: the same price applies
whether a party is long or short, except in quarter hours when the capacity reserve is called.

The reBAP is built from the prices of the balancing energy the transmission system operators
activated, with further components that keep its incentive function. One of them couples it to
intraday prices, so that being out of balance is never cheaper than closing the position on the
intraday market.

For a renewables portfolio the imbalance cost is the forecast error times the gap between the
imbalance price and the day-ahead price the volume was sold at. When the whole system is short
because wind came in below forecast, the imbalance price is high exactly when a wind portfolio
is short too: forecast errors are correlated with the system, which makes them expensive.
